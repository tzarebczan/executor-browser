Executor/Browser

"Patch bay, night shift." The side panel is the bench instrument that patches a remote brain (Executor) into local hands (Chrome). Warm carbon chassis, amber for anything that needs the operator, phosphor green strictly for live. The uplink spine traces current from Executor down into your tabs. This page imports the extension's own stylesheet — what you see here is what ships.

Capture is a local JPEG of the agent tab, for the human only — it is never sent to Executor. Agents take their own screenshot / snapshot through the reverse bridge.
